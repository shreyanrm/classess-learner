# 01 · Locked Product Stances (non-negotiable)

These are decided. Do not relitigate; build to them.

## Pedagogy
- **Build, don't watch.** Universal loop: **pose → struggle → reveal**, never explain-first. Openers are always active.
  Video/reading/podcast are *fading reveals*, never openers. Video is NOT the spine — Wobo annotating a live canvas is
  the primary explanatory modality; short cached nuggets are the library (15–30s typical).
- **Not "learning styles" (VAK/VARK is discredited)** → **adaptive multi-representation**. Switch representation on failure.
- **Simulators & mini-games = parametric, pre-verified templates ONLY.** Never freeform LLM generation in that surface.
- **Assistance ladder** (support fades as competence grows): Learn → Coach → Hint → Work-with-me → Check-my-work → Challenge → Assessment.
- **Mastery is the termination condition.** The loop serves modalities until the band crosses **independent** on unaided Practice evidence.

## Mastery & evidence
- `Mastery = Performance × Reliability × Independence × Difficulty × Recency × Consistency` (multiplicative; **Independence** is the keystone).
- Shown as plain-language **bands** (not-started / emerging / developing / secure / independent), never a raw formula.
- **Ten gap types**: prerequisite, conceptual, procedural, application, retention, language, accuracy, speed, confidence, support-dependency.
- **Evidence Graph**: every conclusion links to its evidence; no permanent conclusion from a single interaction.
- **Predicted scores are honest conditional ranges, never guarantees.**
- **Efficacy instrumentation from line one**: measure pre→post mastery deltas.

## Growth & ethics (engineer compulsion only toward independence)
- The rule that resolves habit-vs-integrity: **engineer compulsion only toward behaviours that make the learner more independent.** Habit and integrity must be the same loop.
- **Spike to onboard, never to retain.** Slot-machine mechanics onboard; ritual retains.
- **Sell aspiration ~80%, fear ~20%.** Show the next self, close and visible.
- Free model = a **behavioural daily meter** (concepts mastered, not a clock). Cut at the liking-peak; **never before a real win that day**.
- **Archetype engine** adapts copy/reward/paywall framing/timing — **never price**. Six archetypes: Competitor, Mastery-Seeker, Exam-Anxious, Ritualist, Belonger, Dabbler.
- Borrow Instagram's machinery (optimal-timing notifications, frictionless first win, next-best-action) — **refuse** infinite scroll, variable reward on consumption, guilt/FOMO notifications, engagement-over-outcome.
