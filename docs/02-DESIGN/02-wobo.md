# 02 · Wobo — the signature tutor (LOCKED) + the Wobo-cute license

Wobo is the face of the product. Wobo's **identity is locked**; Wobo's **choreography is free** (the license below).

## Identity (LOCKED — never alter)
- **Form:** a single **round squircle jelly** (a soft, rounded blob). No star, no other shapes.
- **Color:** **molten only** — shades of molten orange (the #FF4D1A family). Wobo is the one element with an identity
  color of their own, so Wobo is always unmistakably *Wobo*. (Concept colors / ignite use the full playground elsewhere; molten is Wobo's alone.)
- **Surface:** **matte**, with only a whisper of sheen. Not wet, not glossy.
- **Eyes:** two cute eyes with small wet catchlights. Blinks roughly every ~5s. Perks/squishes responsively.
- **Flame-glow (LOCKED):** a warm molten light-glow beneath Wobo that **bobs with Wobo's float and flickers** (broad soft glow +
  brighter core, irregular flicker). This replaces any hard drop shadow. The flame is part of Wobo's identity — it always exists.

## Behaviour (baseline — extend per page under the license)
- Floats bottom-right by default. On tap Wobo **morphs open** into a **frosted floating panel** (not a full-screen drawer) holding
  the transcript + relevant content. Text and voice input. Voice-listening state = **buttery gooey metaballs** (soft, organic —
  never hard rings).
- **Perception:** Wobo sees the learner's work via the **app event/state stream (NOT screen-share)** — a first-class consumer of
  the app's context bus. Atom scope: on-canvas working + spoken Hinglish. Graduated hints, ask-before-tell, never hands the answer.
- **Memory:** persistent per learner (the switching-cost moat).
- (Capabilities/architecture: see `01-ARCHITECTURE/04-ai-fabric-and-gateway.md`.)

## THE WOBO-CUTE LICENSE (standing rule for Claude Code)
Within the locked identity and the motion system, **you have creative freedom to make Wobo delightful, expressive, well-placed,
and beautifully animated on every page** — and you are *expected* to, because you know each page's purpose and can make Wobo fit it.

**You MAY, per page:**
- Choose where Wobo sits and how Wobo enters/exits (peek, drift in, settle, hop to a relevant spot).
- Give Wobo reactions: squish on a correct answer, lean toward the learner's working when "thinking", a small celebratory bob on
  a mastered node, a gentle dim when Wobo is quietly waiting.
- **Play with and even modify the flame for the page's relevance** — make it lean, trail, flare softly on a win, calm to an ember
  while waiting, flicker brighter as a hint escalates. The flame is expressive; use it.
- Compose the motion primitives freshly for Wobo on each surface.

**You MAY NOT (identity is sacred):**
- Change Wobo's color (molten only), Wobo's form (round jelly only), or remove the flame's existence.
- Break the brand tokens, the no-shadow rule, or the motion-meaning rule.
- Make Wobo hand over answers, or perceive via screen-share.

Principle: **freedom in choreography, never in identity.** Surprise the learner on every page; never make Wobo unrecognizable.

## Build note (the one MVP exception)
Wobo is the single exception to "fully functional day one." Wobo must be **transcendent on the atom first** (perception + grounded
reasoning + natural response on linear equations), then widen. Prove perception narrow before widening it.
