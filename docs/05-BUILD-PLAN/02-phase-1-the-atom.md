# Phase 1 · The Atom — Linear Equations in One Variable (THE PROOF GATE)

**Goal:** one topic taught *transcendently* end to end. If this is undeniable, the product works. Everything wide waits on this.

## Build order (within the phase)
1. **The perception-and-grading spike FIRST (keystone, riskiest).** Before polishing UI, prove two things on real working:
   - **Perception:** can Vidya reliably read messy **on-canvas working** + **spoken Hinglish** for linear equations via the app
     event/state stream (NOT screen-share)?
   - **Grading:** does grounded, verifier-checked grading of free working **agree with human judgement**? Stand up the grading
     calibration harness here and measure agreement. **If this spike is weak, stop and report — do not paper over it with UI.**
2. **Ontology seed** for the atom: the node(s) for linear equations in one variable + their prerequisites, in the contract seed.
3. **The Learn loop** for the atom (`04-PAGES/02-learn.md`, `03-FLOWS/02-learn-flow.md`): OpenerCard (interactive via Mafs/JSXGraph),
   CanvasSurface, the pose→struggle→reveal cycle, attempt capture.
4. **Vidya, transcendent on this atom:** all five capabilities live for linear equations — perceive, context-assemble, reason
   (verifier-grounded), respond (sub-second, graduated hints, voice+text, gooey listening), remember. Full **cute-license choreography** on this surface.
5. **Practice/evidence** for the atom: unaided FSRS-scheduled items, grading via verifier + calibration, evidence → mastery factors.
6. **Mastery + ignite + constellation** for the atom: band crosses independent → ignite → light the neighbours.
7. **Efficacy instrumentation:** pre→post delta on the atom captured from line one.

## Definition of "atom proven" (the gate)
- A real learner can be taught linear equations end to end and reach **independent** on unaided evidence.
- Vidya perceives their actual working and tutors without handing answers, at sub-second feel.
- Grading agrees with human judgement within the calibration threshold.
- Nothing unverified was served. Every step emitted its events. Ignite/constellation fire on real mastery.

## Gate (stop here)
Phase report + Phase 1 checklist + the spike's measured results. **This is the most important verification in the whole build.**
Do not generalize to other topics until Shreyan approves the atom.
