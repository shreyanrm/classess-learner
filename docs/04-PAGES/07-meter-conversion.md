# 07 · Meter & Conversion moments

**Purpose:** govern the free day and convert on aspiration at emotional peaks (see `03-FLOWS/08-conversion-and-meter-flow.md`).
**Contains:** MeterSheet (frost; concepts-budget, calm) · contextual conversion moment (aspirational, ~80/20) · founding-member + referral-queue
(scarcity-as-honour) · plan view (Superstar, ₹999/mo · ₹8,999/yr). **Payment itself is the final phase (with auth).**
**States:** budget-open · consuming · real-win-reached · peak(elevated) · cut · conversion-shown · (un-elevated: fixed-fair budget) · subscribed.
**Vidya:** warm, proud at a conversion peak; never pushy; never weaponised. No dark-pattern choreography.
**Events:** `meter.*`, `conversion.moment.shown.v1`, `conversion.completed.v1`.
**AI:** (elevated only) `archetype.classify`, `peakcut.evaluate`; `orchestrator.*` for real-win detection.
**Guardrails (hard):** never cut before a real win that day; archetype never touches price; no behavioural targeting of under-18; weeks 1–2
near-silent; no free trials; no FOMO/guilt; aspiration-led copy, no exclamation marks.
