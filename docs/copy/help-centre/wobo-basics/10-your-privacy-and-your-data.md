# Your privacy and your data

**We collect what is needed to teach you, we never sell it, and you can export or delete all of it whenever you want.**

**What we hold**
Your account details, your board and class, your syllabus edits, your learning history and mastery, your saved boards, and what Wobo remembers about how you learn. Billing details are held by our payment provider, not by us.

**What we do not do**
We do not sell data. We do not advertise, so there is no profile built for advertisers. We do not use a child's learning data to market to them.

**Who else sees it**
Third-party AI and infrastructure providers process what is needed to run Wobo, under contract, and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes. Your parent sees only what the parent link shows, and only if you or they set it up.

**Seeing it**
Settings, data and privacy. The memory page lists what Wobo knows about you in plain sentences. Export gives you a file with everything.

**Deleting it**
Delete any single memory from the memory page. Delete the whole account from the same section: it asks once, then it happens. We delete your account, your learning history and your boards, and it cannot be undone. The only thing we keep is the billing records the law requires us to hold, and backups age out within [n] days.

**If you are under 18**
Some features need a parent's consent before they turn on, including some kinds of memory. Wobo works without them. A parent can review and withdraw consent at any time, and can request deletion on your behalf.
