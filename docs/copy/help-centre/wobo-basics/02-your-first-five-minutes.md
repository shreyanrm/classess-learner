# Your first five minutes

**Sign in, tell Wobo what you are studying, and Wobo teaches you one real thing before asking you for anything else.**

Here is the whole of it.

1. **Sign in.** With your phone number or an email address. Your name comes across with the account, so Wobo knows what to call you.
2. **Tell Wobo what you are studying right now.** Type it, say it, or take a photo of your textbook page or timetable. Something like "class 9 CBSE, we are on quadrilaterals" is plenty.
3. **Confirm your board and class.** Wobo works it out from what you said and shows you one line to tap. If Wobo got it wrong, fix it there.
4. **The first lesson.** Wobo teaches one real thing from what you are actually on this week, on the board, in about two minutes. This is not a demo. It counts.
5. **The tour.** Wobo walks you around the app and points at the real controls: the thread, Learn, Practice, the board, how to circle something and ask about it, how to hold to talk. You try each one as Wobo points.
6. **Three quick questions** so the first analogies land in a world you care about.

That is it. You never see any of this again; coming back drops you straight where you left off.

If you would rather skip ahead, say "skip the tour" at any point. Nothing breaks.
