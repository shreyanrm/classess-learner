# Plans and billing

**Wobo is free to use. Plus removes the daily limit on turns, and the price is the same for everyone.**

**Free**
A real product, not a trial. Your board, your lessons, the board in all three forms, practice, boss battles, your progress and the parent link. What is limited is how many turns you get each day. The counter is on screen and tells you the exact time it resets.

**Plus**
No daily limit on turns. Monthly or annual, and annual costs less per month. [Owner: nothing else is named here until it is decided and built. Any further Plus benefit, and whether there is a family plan and how many seats it carries, is an open decision recorded in `docs/copy/README.md`.]

**What "turns" means**
One turn is one thing you ask Wobo, and everything Wobo draws and says in reply. [Owner: exactly which actions count against the daily limit is an open decision. The budget meter counts every tier, so nothing is described here as free of charge until the metering rule is settled.]

**Paying**
Card, or the payment method your device offers. At checkout you confirm two things in writing: that you accept the terms and the privacy notice, and that you understand the amount, when it renews and how to cancel. You should never be surprised by a charge.

**Renewals**
We write to you 7 days before any renewal, and 30 days before as well if you are on an annual or family plan, with the amount and the date. Cancel any time before it and you are not charged.

**Cancelling**
Settings, then plan, then cancel. We offer you a pause or a smaller plan first; say no and it is done on the next screen. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for, then the account goes back to free with everything you have learnt intact.

**Receipts**
Every payment gets one by email. For anything that looks wrong, write to [support email] with it.
