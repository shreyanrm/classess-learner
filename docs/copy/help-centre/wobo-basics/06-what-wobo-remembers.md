# What Wobo remembers about you

**Wobo remembers your board and class, what you have mastered, the mistakes you keep making and the examples you like. You can see all of it and delete any of it.**

Memory is what makes a tutor useful on day forty. Without it, every lesson starts from nothing.

**What is kept**

- Your board, class, subjects and any edits you made to your syllabus.
- Which topics you have mastered, and whether you did it on your own or with help.
- Mistakes worth re-testing, so Wobo brings them back later rather than letting them settle.
- The worlds your analogies come from, if you told Wobo what you are into.
- Recent conversation, so "do that again with a bigger number" makes sense.
- Boards you saved to your notes.

**What is not kept**

Ink that Wobo draws on a screen and lets fade is not stored. Voice is used to understand you in the moment and is not kept as a recording. Wobo does not build a profile for advertising, because there is no advertising.

**Seeing and deleting it**

Settings has a memory page. Everything Wobo knows about you is listed there in plain sentences, and each line has a delete next to it. Deleting a line takes effect on your next turn.

On a learner account under 18, some memory is off until a parent has given consent. Wobo works either way; it just remembers less.
