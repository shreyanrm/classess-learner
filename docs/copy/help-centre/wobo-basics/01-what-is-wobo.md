# What is Wobo

**Wobo is a tutor who explains things by drawing them, live, on your own syllabus.**

Ask Wobo anything and the answer arrives as ink. A pointer at the part of the diagram you asked about. A graph built while Wobo talks you through it. A derivation written out line by line in Wobo's own handwriting.

Wobo is on every screen in the app. On the home screen Wobo is the conversation. Everywhere else Wobo is the small orb in the corner, watching the screen with you and one tap from drawing on it.

Three things make Wobo different from a video or a search box:

- **It is your syllabus.** You pick your board and class, and Wobo teaches that, chapter by chapter, in its order.
- **It is drawn for you, now.** Nothing is pre-recorded. If you ask the same question tomorrow with a different example, you get a different drawing.
- **Every number is checked before it is drawn.** The maths is computed and verified by code, not remembered. If a check fails, Wobo redraws it or tells you.

Wobo is free to use. A Plus plan gives you more turns and a few extras; the free tier is a real product, not a trailer.
