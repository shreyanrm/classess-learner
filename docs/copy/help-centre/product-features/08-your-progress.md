# Your progress

**Your progress page is Wobo's honest account of what you know, written and drawn by Wobo, not a dashboard of numbers.**

**What is on it**

- **This week, in Wobo's words.** A short summary of what you actually did, including the useful things that are not scores. "You asked me why six times this week, which means you are working through problems rather than skipping them."
- **The map.** Everything you have studied, drawn as a map that lights up. It shows two different states: what you can do on your own, and what you can do with help. They are not the same thing and we do not pretend they are.
- **Problems solved**, week, month or year.
- **Strengths, based on behaviour.** Resilience, for asking after a wrong answer. Initiative, for asking on your own. These come from what you did, not from a personality test.
- **What to revisit**, and why, with a link to the moment it came from.

**Everything links back to its evidence**

Any claim on the page opens the thing that proves it: the question, the board, the day. If Wobo says you have mastered something, you can see exactly why Wobo thinks so.

**Sharing it**

The map is built to be screenshotted. The parent link shares this page read-only, if you set one up.

**Ask it questions**

Ask Wobo "what am I weakest at" or "what do I need before astrophysics" and Wobo answers from this page and draws the path.
