# The plane

**The plane is a frosted board that slides out over whatever you are doing, so Wobo can work something out without taking you off the page.**

Say "board", or ask something that needs real working, and the plane arrives from the orb. It is see-through, so the thing you were looking at stays visible underneath.

**What you can do with it**

- **Move it, resize it, pin it.** Drag it where it is not in the way. Pin it and it stays while you scroll.
- **Shrink it.** It becomes a small thumbnail near the orb with all its ink intact. Tap to bring it back exactly as it was.
- **Draw on it.** Your strokes and Wobo's share the same surface. Scribble your working and Wobo reads it.
- **Wipe it.** Say "wipe" or use the eraser. Say "fresh board" and you get a second one; you can have several in a session.

**On a phone**

The plane comes up as a sheet from the bottom. Drag it up for more room, down to shrink it, and it keeps its ink either way.

**What lives on it**

Anything that takes more than a few strokes: a derivation written out, a graph built from the axes up, a free-body diagram, a molecule drawn in the order a chemist would draw it. Move a slider Wobo has drawn and every number that depends on it is recomputed and rewritten.

**Keeping it**

The plane's ink stays until you wipe it. Save it to your notes and it is yours permanently.
