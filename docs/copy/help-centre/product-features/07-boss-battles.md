# Boss battles

**A boss battle is the interactive workbook that closes a topic. Finish it and the topic counts as done.**

It is one continuous piece of work rather than a list of questions: a real problem, drawn on the board, that you solve on the same surface, with the parts building on each other.

**How it goes**

- Wobo sets it up on the board and steps back.
- You work. Write, drag, shade, build, draw your working beside it.
- Wobo marks the working, not only the answer, and ink lands on the step that went wrong.
- Help is available throughout and does not disqualify anything.
- At the end you get Wobo's closing word on how it went and your XP.

**If you do not finish**

Leave and come back. Your place, your working and your ink are all saved. Nothing is lost and nothing resets.

**The synthesis boss**

Once you have a few topics mastered, Wobo builds a bigger one that crosses them, assembled from what you personally know, so it connects things nobody told you were connected. It arrives when you have earned it, not on a schedule.

**What it does to your progress**

Beating a boss on your own lights the topic as mastered on your progress page. Beating it with a lot of help lights it as mastered-with-support, which is honest and which is what Wobo uses to decide what to revisit.
