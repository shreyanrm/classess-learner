# Subjects, chapters and topics

**Learn and Practice both open to your subjects. Tap a subject for its chapters, tap a chapter for its topics. A topic is one course.**

**Learn**
Your subjects, as a grid. Inside a subject, your board's chapters in your board's order. Inside a chapter, the topics. Opening a topic starts the lesson on the full board and ends with the boss.

Below the syllabus grid sits anything you asked Wobo to build for you that is not on your syllabus, under "courses". Finished topics move out of the grid onto a "past" shelf, which is a place to look back at, not a list of chores.

**Practice**
The same subjects, for working rather than learning: mixed practice across a chapter, flashcards on anything due for revision, and a free-play area where you can build whatever you like on a topic with no task set and Wobo watching.

**Getting there faster**
You do not have to navigate at all. Tell Wobo "practise circles" or "start trigonometry" from anywhere and Wobo takes you there. On a laptop, the command palette reaches any of it from the keyboard.

**Nothing generated in bulk**
Chapters appear when you pick your board, topics when you open a chapter, and the lesson when you open the topic. If a list looks briefly empty, that is Wobo fetching it, not a missing syllabus.
