# Editing your syllabus

**You can add, remove, rename and reorder anything in your syllabus, and your changes survive every update.**

Schools do not follow the printed syllabus exactly. Yours can say what your teacher actually does.

**What you can change**

- Add a chapter or a topic your school teaches that the board does not list.
- Mark something as "not in my school". It stays visible but greyed, out of your plan and out of your progress.
- Rename anything to the name your teacher uses.
- Reorder chapters into the order you are being taught in.
- Attach your textbook so examples match your book.

**How it is stored**

Your edits sit on top of the official version rather than changing it. That matters for two reasons: everyone else still sees the official one, and when the board publishes a new version your edits are reapplied to it automatically.

**When something no longer fits**

If the board renames or removes a chapter you had edited, Wobo tells you exactly which of your changes no longer has a home and asks what you want done with it. One line per change.

**Starting again**

There is a reset that removes all your edits and returns you to the official version. It asks once.
