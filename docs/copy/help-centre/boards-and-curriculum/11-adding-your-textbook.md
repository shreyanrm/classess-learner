# Adding your textbook

**Tell Wobo which book you use and the examples, notation and order match the one on your desk.**

**How to add it**

On any subject, open its settings and add your textbook: photograph the cover and the contents page, or upload the PDF. You can also attach your teacher's notes or a school scheme of work the same way.

**What changes**

- Chapter names and order follow your book where they differ from the board's document.
- Notation follows your book. If your book writes a formula a particular way, Wobo writes it that way.
- Examples reference the kind your book uses, so the practice you do here looks like the exercises you are set.

**What does not change**

The truth. Wobo will not teach something incorrect because a book prints it that way. If your book differs from the board's syllabus on a point that matters, Wobo shows both and says which the board examines.

**Several books**

Add more than one. Wobo uses the one you marked as your main book for notation and mentions the others where they explain something better.

**Removing it**

Remove it from the same place. Your progress is unaffected.
