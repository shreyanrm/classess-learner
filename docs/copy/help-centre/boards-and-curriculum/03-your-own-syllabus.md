# Using your own syllabus

**Show Wobo your syllabus and Wobo builds a plan around it. Photo, PDF, paste or type.**

Use this when your board is not published anywhere, when you are homeschooled, when your school has its own scheme of work, or when you simply want to study a fixed list of things.

**Four ways in**

- **A photo.** The syllabus page in your book, a printed sheet, or the timetable stuck to your wall. Take it in reasonable light and get the whole page in frame.
- **A PDF.** Upload it.
- **Paste.** Copy it from wherever it lives.
- **Type it.** Chapter names on separate lines is enough. You do not need topics; Wobo can propose those and you confirm.

**Confirming it**

Wobo turns it into a structure and shows it to you unit by unit. One tap per unit to confirm, rename or delete. This takes a minute and it is the step that makes everything after it correct.

**What it is labelled**

Drafted from your syllabus, check it. Because we know exactly where it came from: you.

**It is yours**

A syllabus you built is private to your account and is not shared with anyone. If you want to offer it to other learners at your school, there is a control to share it; it then goes through a check before anyone else sees it. That is entirely optional and off by default.
