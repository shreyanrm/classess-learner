# Your board is not in the list

**Type it anyway. Wobo goes and looks for the official syllabus, reads it, checks it, and builds it, usually while you make tea.**

The list is not the limit. Below the search there is always a line saying your board might not be there yet, and it never takes more than one tap.

**What Wobo does**

1. Searches for the official syllabus document, starting with the board's own site.
2. Reads it, and turns the chapter and topic structure into something Wobo can teach from, keeping a link to the page it came from for every single item.
3. Checks the result independently against the same document: do the chapter counts match the document's own contents page, are any topics missing or duplicated, is the order right.
4. Saves it, with the source attached, and shows it to you straight away.

**What you see**

The syllabus, with a label saying it was found on the board's site and is still being checked. That label is honest: it means the structure came from a real document and has passed our checks, but no human has confirmed it yet. Read "How we label a syllabus".

**If Wobo cannot find anything official**

Wobo says so in one line rather than making something up, and offers to build it from your own syllabus instead. Wobo will never invent a chapter list and present it as your board's.

**For the next person**

Once your board is in, the next learner from your school gets it instantly. It is worth the wait.
