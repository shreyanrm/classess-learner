# What Wobo covers

**School subjects, from about class 4 to class 12 or 13, on whichever board you study, in English.**

**Levels**
From class 4 up to class 12 or 13, wherever a board has them. Below class 4 and above school level, we do not pretend. We would rather do school properly than do everything badly.

**Subjects**
Whatever your board sets for your class. In most cases that means mathematics, science or the separate physics, chemistry and biology, social science or its separate subjects, and the language and skill subjects your board defines. Wobo uses your board's own names and its own grouping.

**What Wobo is best at**
Anything that can be drawn, computed, simulated or argued out on a board: mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, geography, economics, and the reasoning parts of everything else.

**What Wobo is honest about**
Wobo is not a marking scheme and does not know your school's internal question papers. For a subject that turns on your teacher's own notes, attach them and Wobo works from those.

**Language**
English first. Say things to Wobo in your own language mid-sentence and Wobo will follow. Your board's names for things are kept in the original alongside the English, so nothing gets lost.

**Beyond the syllabus**
Once a topic is done, Wobo sometimes offers a hidden lesson on something genuinely interesting just outside it. Optional, and worth taking.
