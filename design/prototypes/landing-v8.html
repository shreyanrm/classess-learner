Safe by design

A tutor a ten-year-old talks to alone has to be built differently.

Not a promise page. Six decisions, each one visible in the product, each one checkable.

Your child's questions are not a product

No ads, no tracking pixels, no third-party cookies, nothing sold in aggregate. The company is paid by families, so there is nothing to monetise sideways.

Read what we hold →
Neutral on everything but the chapter

Politics, religion, anything contested: Wobo names the textbook's position and steers back. It has no opinions to give a child.

The neutral rule →
A parent sees the learning, not the diary

Lessons, progress and the Sunday note, always. Questions word for word only if the child shares them. Trust runs both ways or it is not trust.

Who sees what →
Encrypted, and locked at the row

Everything travels over TLS and sits encrypted at rest, with access rules inside the database itself: a learner's rows are reachable only by that learner, even if our own code slipped.

How it is protected →
Erase everything, in one tap

Memory, progress, account. Gone from live systems at once and from backups inside thirty days, for the learner or a linked parent.

The erase button →
No child's work trains anything

Questions and drawings are never used to train a model unless a parent turns that on, explicitly, and it can be turned off again the same day.

Questions

What families ask first.

Will it just hand my child the answers?

No. Wobo shows the reasoning and stops at the step your child has to take. When they are close it rings the gap on their own working and waits. There is nothing to copy, because the answer does not exist until the thinking is done.

Does it follow our school's syllabus?

Yes. You pick the board and class once, and Wobo teaches the chapter your class is on, in your textbook's order. If your school does something differently, tell Wobo and it reorders, adds or drops a chapter.

Is it safe for a ten-year-old alone?

It is built for exactly that. Wobo stays inside school subjects, holds no opinions on anything contested, shows no ads, and never makes a child feel small. Voice is not stored. The whole of it is written out on the security page.

What does free actually include?

The whole tutor: every subject, the drawn board, the films, the practice, the memory and the Sunday note, with a daily allowance of questions that resets every morning. Paid plans raise the allowance for exam season. They do not unlock the teacher.

Which languages does it speak?

English today, with the accent set by where you are. More languages follow the boards that ask for them.
