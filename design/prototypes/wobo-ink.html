Ink Wobo — black on paper in light, paper on ink in dark

One body, one visor, one colour. The body is ink in light and paper in dark; the visor is the opposite; the eyes are always ultramarine, the only pigment on the screen. A half-pixel hairline in the opposite tone keeps her legible over any content, including the frosted plane over a dark video. Warmth comes from the eyes and the motion, not from colour.
