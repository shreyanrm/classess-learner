How it works

Scroll, and she teaches you Pythagoras.

You control the pace. This is exactly what happens inside Wobo, on any question, on any topic in your syllabus.

Start with what you can see

She draws a right triangle and names the sides as she says them. No formula yet.

a, b… and the long one is c

Build the squares

A square on each side, drawn stroke by stroke. Now the question is about areas, not letters.

watch the big one

Then the formula appears, earned

The two small squares fit exactly into the big one. She writes the line only once you've seen it.

a² + b² = c²

Your turn

She hands you the pen. Circle a step, ask why, drag a corner. Her numbers update because they are computed, not remembered.
