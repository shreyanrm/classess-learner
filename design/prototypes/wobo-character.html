Wobo, the wobot — three directions

Same molten body colour, same eyes-do-the-acting rule. Each has one signature feature. Left to right: idle · thinking · celebrating. Bottom: the 16 px and 32 px silhouette test in ink.

A · Spark

A soft teardrop body, slightly heavier at the bottom, with the brand's ultramarine spark on a short antenna. The spark is her thought: it glows when she thinks, bursts when she gets it. The wordmark and the character share one mark.
