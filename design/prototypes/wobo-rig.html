Wobo rig — expressions, idle life, reactions

Move the mouse and she looks and leans. Hover: she perks up. Click: squash and a smile. Double-click: hop. Hold: listening. Drag her around. Leave her alone: she glances about, gets bored, yawns, dozes off, and wakes with a start when you come back. T toggles the theme.
idle
Idle life: after 4 s she glances around; 12 s bored (half lids, slow sway); 20 s a yawn or a sigh; 35 s dozes (lids close, slow breath); any motion wakes her with a surprised blink. Random double-blinks throughout. Reactions: hover perks her up and she leans in; click squashes her and she smiles; double-click hops; hold turns her to listening; drag carries her, body stretching toward where she's going; release settles with a wobble.
